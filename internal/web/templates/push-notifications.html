Push Notifications

Subscribe this browser to a topic under your user ID. Anyone who knows both the user ID and the topic can send a notification to it, even when this page is closed. Treat the topic like a password.

Using an iPhone or iPad?

iOS only delivers notifications to websites added to the Home Screen. It needs iOS / iPadOS 16.4 or later.

  1. Open this page in Safari. Other iPhone browsers can't add it.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open the new Push icon on your Home Screen, not Safari.
  4. Tap Subscribe and allow notifications when asked.

The Home Screen app has its own storage, so it starts with a new user ID. Paste your old one in to keep it. Removing the app from the Home Screen also removes its subscription.
